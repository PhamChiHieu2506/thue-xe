CAP NHAT GIA THUE / DIA DIEM HIEN THI

1. Mo Docker Desktop va doi Engine running.
2. Trong thu muc du an cu, chay:
   docker compose --profile balance down
   Khong them -v; giu nguyen .env, .local va cac volume du lieu.
3. Chep source moi vao thu muc du an. Can cap nhat ca thu muc carmanage-service
   (Java, JS, CSS va tai nguyen TSV), khong chi chep rieng giao dien.
4. Chay: docker compose up -d --build
5. Mo http://localhost:8080 va nhan Ctrl+F5.
