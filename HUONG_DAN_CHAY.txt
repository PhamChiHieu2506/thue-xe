HUONG DAN CHAY VELODRIVE - WINDOWS / DOCKER

A. NANG CAP TU BAN CU (GIU DU LIEU)
1. Mo Docker Desktop, doi Engine running.
2. Mo thu muc du an CU dang chay, noi co docker-compose.yml, .env va .local.
3. Mo PowerShell tai day. Chay: docker compose --profile balance down
   Lenh nay dung va xoa container de tao lai; KHONG them -v. Volume du lieu van giu.
4. Sao luu du lieu va thu muc .local/.env; chep de source moi vao dung thu muc cu.
   Khong xoa .env, .local, keys va cac volume trong Docker Desktop.
5. Chay: .\scripts\00a_setup_local.cmd
   Lenh chi bo sung cau hinh con thieu, trong do co mat khau DB Rental moi.
   Khong doi key, mat khau DB Auth/Car hoac tai khoan nguoi dung da co.
   File tao demo cu con sot khi chep de duoc chuyen vao .local\retired-source,
   khong con duoc bien dich. Neu dung Eclipse, Project -> Clean sau cap nhat.
6. Chay: docker compose up -d --build
7. Chay: docker compose ps
   Cho tat ca dich vu Up (healthy). Lan build dau co the mat vai phut.
8. Mo http://localhost:8080/dashboard.html va nhan Ctrl+F5.
9. Dang nhap bang tai khoan cu. Xe cu da gui cho duyet va du thong tin se duoc
   dang tu dong. Xe thieu thong tin: Xe cua toi -> Chinh sua -> bo sung -> Luu
   thay doi. Khong can admin duyet. Xe mau cu khong hien thi, khong tu sinh lai.

B. CAI MOI
1. Cai JDK 21; mo PowerShell chay java -version de kiem tra.
2. Mo Docker Desktop den khi Engine running.
3. Giai nen source. Vao thu muc co docker-compose.yml, giu Shift + chuot phai,
   chon Open PowerShell/Terminal here, hoac bam thanh dia chi go powershell.
4. Chay: .\scripts\00a_setup_local.cmd
5. Chay: docker compose up -d --build
6. Chay: docker compose ps
7. Mo http://localhost:8080 . Eureka: http://localhost:8761 .
8. Mo .local\TAI_KHOAN_QUAN_TRI.txt de xem ten va mat khau admin khoi tao.
   Mat khau trong tep nay chi ap dung khi CSDL chua co ADMIN.
9. Dang nhap admin, vao Ho so de doi mat khau cua ban.
10. Owner va customer tu dang ky tren web; chon dung vai tro khi dang ky.

C. DANG MOT XE CHO THUE - KHONG QUA DUYET
1. Dang ky tai khoan Chu xe. Dang nhap -> Xe cua toi -> Dang xe moi.
2. Chon dong xe de dien nhanh ten/hang/so cho va dung anh co san; hoac tu nhap.
3. Nhap bien so, nam san xuat, so cho, gia/24 gio, hop so, nhien lieu,
   tinh/thanh pho, phuong/xa, dia chi nhan va mo ta/yeu cau thue.
4. Co the tai 1-6 anh JPEG/PNG cua xe (toi da 8 MB/anh); anh tai len duoc uu tien.
   Xe tu nhap khong co anh trong danh muc thi can it nhat mot anh tai len.
5. Chon San sang cho thue -> bam Them va dang xe. Xe hien ngay cho customer khi
   tim dung dia diem va lich con trong. Khong co buoc Gui duyet hay Admin duyet.
6. Chinh sua -> Luu thay doi: xe van duoc dang. Luu tru se an xe khoi tim kiem.
7. Xe cu chua dang: bo sung thong tin roi Luu thay doi; hoac bam Dang xe neu da du.
   Yeu cau thue van can chu xe xac nhan de giu lich; do la quy trinh chuyen xe.

D. KHACH DAT XE
1. Customer dang ky tai khoan Khach thue. Dang nhap.
2. Chon dia diem, thoi gian nhan/tra trong tuong lai. Bam Tim xe phu hop.
3. Xem xe -> Kiem tra lich & chi phi -> doc tong tien, so ngay, khoan giu cho.
4. Dong y quy trinh va Gui yeu cau thue xe. Yeu cau o trang thai Cho chu xe duyet.
5. Owner vao Yeu cau & chuyen xe -> Chi tiet -> Duyet yeu cau; nhap thong tin ban giao.
6. Customer mo Chi tiet chuyen. Sau khi owner duyet, xem huong dan thanh toan.
   Neu chuyen khoan: thuc hien chuyen tien that truoc, roi gui so tien va ma giao dich.
   Neu tien mat: tra truc tiep cho owner; owner ghi nhan so tien da thuc nhan.
7. Owner kiem tra ngan hang, chi xac nhan khi tien da ve. Gui ma giao dich KHONG tu
   dong bien thanh da thanh toan. Du khoan giu cho -> Da xac nhan.
8. Dia chi ban giao hien cho customer sau khi chuyen da xac nhan.
9. Trong vong 2 gio truoc lich nhan, owner xac nhan da ban giao xe -> Dang thue.
10. Thu phan tien con lai va ghi nhan giao dich. Khi nhan lai xe, owner xac nhan
    tra xe & hoan tat. He thong chi hoan tat khi tien thue da du.

E. CAU HINH NHAN TIEN / HUY / HOAN TIEN
- Owner -> Nhan thanh toan -> Chon Tien mat hoac Chuyen khoan, nhap thong tin dung.
- So tai khoan duoc che o trang cai dat; ben lien quan xem day du trong chuyen da duyet.
- Tranh nhap ma giao dich gia: day la so ghi nhan/doi soat, khong ket noi ngan hang tu dong.
- Truoc ban giao co the Huy chuyen va neu ly do. He thong luu so tien can hoan.
- Owner thuc hien hoan tien o ngoai he thong, sau do ghi ma giao dich hoan that.
- Yeu cau da duyet qua han thanh toan duoc giai phong lich; job chuyen trang thai moi phut.
- Khoan giu cho mac dinh 40%, han toi da 24h va khong qua gio nhan xe. Thay doi trong
  .env: BOOKING_DEPOSIT_PERCENT, BOOKING_HOLD_MINUTES; chay docker compose up -d de ap dung.

F. KIEM TRA VAN HANH
- Danh sach container: docker compose ps
- Log Rental: docker compose logs --tail=100 rental-service
- Trang thai Auth: http://localhost:8082/actuator/health/readiness
- Trang thai Car: http://localhost:8080/actuator/health/readiness
- Trang thai Rental: http://localhost:8081/actuator/health/readiness
- Dung 1 dich vu: docker compose stop carmanage-service
- Khoi dong lai: docker compose start carmanage-service
- Xem can bang tai: docker compose --profile balance up -d --build
  Replica Car o localhost:8090; Rental goi theo ten dich vu trong Eureka.
- Dung he thong: docker compose --profile balance down (khong them -v).

G. LOI THUONG GAP
- Trang tim xe co 21 dong xe de lua chon. Chon dong xe, tinh/thanh va lich de tim
  xe da duoc dang. Xe co gia va lich trong la xe owner da dang du thong tin.
  Neu chua co xe cua dong do tai khu vuc da chon, thu doi dia diem hoac lich.
- Mat khau admin trong tep moi khong dung sau nang cap: dung mat khau admin cu;
  bootstrap khong dat lai tai khoan da co.
- DB password authentication failed: kiem tra .env cu tuong ung volume, khong sinh
  lai mat khau khi volume da co du lieu.
- HTTP 409 trung lich: yeu cau khac vua duoc duyet, chon thoi gian/xe khac.
- HTTP 503 khi bao gia: Car/Eureka chua san sang; xem health/log va doi dang ky.
- HTTP 401: phien JWT het han (15 phut), dang nhap lai.
- Tai anh loi: dung PNG/JPEG, 200x150 tro len, khong qua 8 MB va khong qua 40 MP.
- Khong can chay cac Main trong Eclipse neu da chay Docker (tranh trung cong).

2. O Dia diem nhan xe, chon trong 34 tinh/thanh hien hanh; de Toan quoc neu tim moi noi.
3. Customer co 21 dong xe kem anh, co the loc hang/so cho va bam Chon dong xe.
4. Bam Tim xe dong nay de xem xe dang cho thue tai khu vuc va lich da chon.
   Chi xe da dang moi co gia va nut xem chi tiet/bao gia; khong sinh chu xe/gia gia dinh.
5. Owner mo Dang xe moi: co the chon dong xe de dien nhanh ten/hang/so cho va xem anh.
   Nhap du dia diem, gia va cac truong bat buoc; bam Them va dang xe de hien ngay.
6. Xe cu chua co anh da co anh theo dong xe tren the. Khong doi ID, bien so, chu so huu,
   gia hoac tu gan anh upload vao ban ghi cu. Trang thai cho duyet cu du thong tin
   duoc chuyen sang da dang; xe nhap/chua du thong tin giu rieng.
